# Limitations

- Canonical DAWN full-text, implementation and wire-vector validation is incomplete.
- BAT's 2026 proof correction has not been compared theorem by theorem with its earlier version.
- Reproducing the DAWN independence-model arithmetic does not prove actual correlated, per-key or adversarial-input DFR.
- No comprehensive independent lattice-estimator campaign or quantum ISD campaign was run.
- No physical quantum-computer experiment was performed.
- MIKE's concrete independent quantum margin remains unresolved.
- A complete project-audited MIKE IND-CCA KEM and ciphertext encoding were unavailable.
- No complete same-platform RAM, stack and runtime comparison exists.
- Publication CI checks numerical reproducibility only.

The findings are scoped to the examined design space and evidence available through 27 September 2026. They do not prove ML-KEM optimality or rule out future compact KEMs.
