# Cross-family comparison — source-version-aware byte ledger

**Unit:** bytes. PK+CT is one recipient public key plus one transmitted ciphertext. “Raw” denotes a NIKE/object exchange, **not** an IND-CCA KEM. UNKNOWN is never zero. Level labels are scheme-specific claims and do not make all rows equally reviewed.
