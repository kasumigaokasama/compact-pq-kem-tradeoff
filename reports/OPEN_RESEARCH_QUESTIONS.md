# Open mathematical and security questions

These are questions, **not** proposed KEMs or parameter choices.

1. **Compact average-case relation.** Can a short public instance support efficient secret-assisted key recovery while resisting canonical-form, projection, equivalent-key and hidden-symmetry attacks on its *sampled* distribution? A positive answer requires a precise sampler and asymmetry, not generic NP-hardness.
2. **Public compression versus structural attack.** Within a fixed scheme family, can one relate bits removed by ring/module/QC structure to extra automorphism, distinguisher or multi-target advantage? Family-specific conditional lower bounds may be feasible; no universal 1-KB lower bound was established.
3. **Zero- or negligible-failure compact decoder.** Can BIKE-like one-syndrome communication obtain a rigorous *per-key* extreme-tail bound and constant-time reaction resistance without exposing a sparse dual? HQC's larger public-code region and current QC-MDPC DFR studies define the comparison.
4. **Correlated DAWN DFR.** Can one derive a rigorous generating-function, importance-sampling or analytical upper bound preserving fixed-weight negacyclic products, shared key variables, paired decoder positions, rounding and message conditioning? Does it preserve an acceptable QROM loss?
5. **BAT correction.** Which lemma/game hop changed in the 12 April 2026 proof, and do corrected hypotheses and parameters still cover BAT-512? Requires two immutable PDFs and a dependency-tree diff.
6. **Quantum costs in real resources.** How should lattice sieving, quantum ISD and hidden-shift algorithms be compared when oracle T gates, depth, QRAM, logical/physical qubits and large classical preprocessing differ?
7. **Module-action cryptanalysis.** Does MIKE's monoidal action avoid all *useful* hidden-shift-style quantum formulations? What are best independent classical/quantum attacks on endomorphism/path recovery and parallelised shared secret for its exact distribution?
8. **MIKE CCA transform.** Can a complete IND-CCA KEM retain the 64 B public object and sub-1-KB PK+CT at the same security parameter, with validation, deterministic reencryption or alternative simulation, rejection, QROM reduction and low loss? The question presumes no answer.
9. **Validation metadata.** How much information must an untrusted curve/object ciphertext transmit or prove for efficient domain membership without leaking torsion, orientation or related shifted curves?
10. **Reusability and amortization.** How do public-key reuse, many-user attacks and protocol authentication shift the relevant Pareto frontier? A per-encapsulation 96 B McEliece ciphertext does not eliminate initial key distribution.
