# Constrained NTRU research-gap screen

No mechanism currently meets the Phase 2C **GO** threshold. The following are *falsification agendas*, not constructions or selected Phase 3 parameters. In particular, no claim of security, ≥5% real savings, or CCA conversion is made.

| Agenda | Mathematical change and hoped effect | Honest recovery / assumption / new attack surface | Immediate falsification |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1: change message quotient and decoder radius | Encode a message in a quotient with a stronger recoverable residue so coarser ciphertext rounding becomes possible; target ≥49 B on a one-key/one-ciphertext pair | Must prove the centered-lift carry and correlated rounding errors fit a decoder and preserve a viable CCA transform; quotient may leak a projection or produce weak keys | Derive exact decoder and dependency-aware DFR for the proposed quotient; evaluate projection and equivalent-key attacks at the smaller alphabet |
| M2: alter secret-assisted recovery | Use more of the receiver's short relation to tolerate larger compression noise without increasing failure; target ≥49 B | Recovery must hold for the full key distribution with controlled worst-key tails; may introduce a stronger trapdoor assumption or equivalent-key attack | Exhibit full integer-to-modular proof and bound key-conditioned failures, then compare sparse/hybrid and lattice estimates |
| M3: remove redundant public relation | Reduce the public-key object by ≥49 B through a mathematically constrained relation, not entropy coding | Generation and decapsulation need a reliable relation; extra algebra may enable CRT filtering, distinguishers or amortized multi-user attacks | Count the actual relation entropy and search for projected distinguishers/recovery before choosing parameters |

Closest prior art for all three remains the DAWN double-encoding idea, BAT's compact NTRU/trapdoor approach, and established NTRU quotients; a specific novelty claim cannot be made without the canonical DAWN and corrected BAT papers. None has an actual byte count under an explicit valid parameter choice. M1 is particularly constrained by the reproduced author-model rounding sensitivity; M2 and M3 have no surviving preliminary attack estimate. **Zero mechanisms survived preliminary falsification at the ≥5% threshold.** At ≥10%, the 97-byte target is still farther away. The present evidence supports **NO** to constructing an NTRU-family candidate in Phase 3, even though the formal source-validation verdict remains insufficient evidence.
